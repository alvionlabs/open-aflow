2. Key features

  • See an urgent item from another run. If another run’s Next step card ranks higher than yours (blocked, then merge, then reply, then an urgent pending entry), one line shows above the run header: status, title, repo and run name, and +N more.
  • Open that run. “Open run” is a link to the run. After a plain click, focus moves to that run’s title. The action (Answer, Merge) stays on that run’s own card.
  • Nothing changes otherwise. With no run above yours, the slot is empty and takes no space.

Reverse “no card across all runs”, as a banner

Show another run’s urgent item on screen 1, as one line, not a card.
Why
“If it’s urgent, I think the B should be the right way to show for user.”
Instead of
Keep calm-dashboard’s turn-down; a full card across all runs.
Result
The selected run’s card keeps the page’s one primary button.

Rank and tie rules

Decision
Only each run’s card counts. Blocked > merge > reply > pending (now, decision, before) > nothing; strictly higher only. Ties: oldest entry date, then sidebar order.
Why
The banner, the card and the dots read the same nextSteps.
Instead of
Rank by every waiting item, or by the run’s state dot.
Result
A needs you dot with only someday entries raises no banner.

Navigation only, no dismiss, screen 1 only

Decision
“Open run” is the only control; the banner leaves when the item is handled.
Why
Actions stay in one place; a dismiss could hide a real block.
Instead of
Answer or Merge in the banner; a dismiss or snooze; the banner on the wave board.
Result
Two ideas parked: dismiss, other screens.
