# Idea: Dismiss or snooze the urgent banner

Status: parked

## The idea
Let you hide the urgent banner from plan `next-step-across-runs` for a while (for example until the item changes, or for 30 minutes) without handling the item.

## Why it's on hold
The banner is for urgent items only and leaves on its own once the item is handled or no longer outranks the selected run, so a dismiss could hide a real block.

## What would make it worth building
The banner stays up for an item you have deliberately decided to leave for later, and it gets in the way of work on the selected run.
