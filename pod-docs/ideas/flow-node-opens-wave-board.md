# Idea: Click a wave step in the flow to open the wave board

Status: parked

## The idea
On the run screen's flow diagram, the sprint step ("Wave 1 · 3 slices") becomes a link to that run's wave board, so you click the node you are looking at. The human liked this while picking where "Open the wave board" goes (option 3 c in `pod-docs/design-drafts/auto-continue-sign-choices-2.html`, 2026-11-14): "c is interesting, we can click in the node and it opens the waveboard too". Other nodes could open their own detail later, for example Final review opening its PR.

## Why it's on hold
The human picked 3 a for now: the link sits at the right end of the progress row. A clickable node needs a clear hover and focus look on the flow cards, and a decision on what each other node opens.

## What would make it worth building
The flow diagram gets its next change, or users try to click the nodes.
