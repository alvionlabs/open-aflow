1. The choice: where does the dot go?

Words such as blocked and update pod have different widths. So one layout can line up the dots and the word edge only if the dot comes last. The second layout keeps the dot first, like the rest of the app, but the words end at different places.

A. Dot after the word (in the PR now)

Dots and the right edge both line up. It differs from the Next step card, which reads ● Blocked.

B. Dot first, fixed column

Dots line up, and it matches the rest of the app. The words end at different places, so some cards have space on the right.

2. Try it: drag the width, hover a card, open the ⋯

Hover a card to see the ⋯. Click it, then hover Remove repo. Click that to see the confirm. Tab and Enter work too. Below 246 px the cards stack.
